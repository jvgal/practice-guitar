CONNECTING THE SHAPES — up the neck
Uses the scale and root chosen above. Climbs (and descends) the scale across all 5 positions of the neck, low to high — the exercise of linking one shape into the next. The current position lights up bright, the rest of the map stays dim.

CYCLE OF 4THS — exercise
Starts on the key and shape chosen above (e.g. G in the E shape) and walks the cycle G→C→F→B♭… changing the shape along with it E→D→C→A→G… It switches by itself in time (a cue chord marks the change), and you play the scale over it on the metronome cell.
